About The Mountain

History, culture, and geography of Thabana Ntlenyana.

Thabana Ntlenyana is the highest mountain in Southern Africa, reaching approximately 3,482 meters above sea level in the Maloti mountain range of Lesotho. The mountain is known for its breathtaking alpine landscape, cold climate, and sweeping views across the highlands.

The name “Thabana Ntlenyana” translates from Sesotho as “Little Handsome Mountain.” Despite its modest name, the mountain represents one of the most iconic natural landmarks in Lesotho and symbolizes the strength and endurance of the Basotho people.

Travelers often reach the mountain through Sani Pass, one of the most famous mountain roads in Southern Africa. The surrounding highlands are home to shepherd communities, traditional Basotho culture, and landscapes that become covered in snow during winter.

This VR experience was created to digitally preserve and share the beauty of Lesotho's mountain environments while allowing users from around the world to explore the atmosphere and scenery interactively.
